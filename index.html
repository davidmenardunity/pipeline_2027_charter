Unity Pipeline

→ Create games faster ←

Opening Unity production beyond a single seat of the Editor — for every person, tool, and agent on the team.

The Problem

One Editor seat for every interaction

Setting up a Unity production where producers, artists, designers, external partners — even automated systems and AI agents — can work concurrently and safely is tough. The Editor was never designed as everyone's entry point.

  • Many team members don't have the expertise to get valuable results out of the Editor.
  • Onboarding an internal or external collaborator is daunting.
  • Teams have limited ability to automate or extend tooling that scales reliably in production.
  • Result: pipeline managers restrict Editor licenses, and the value/cost perception of a Unity seat suffers.

What Is It

APIs and tools that open a production beyond the Editor

  • Lets studios build an entire production pipeline, A to Z, for everyone on the team.
  • Decouples interactions from the Unity Editor — web, desktop, mobile, or automated.
  • Defines a contract for interactions between people, software, agents, and data.

Five Pillars

Iteration time from minutes to seconds

1

The Ecosystem

Pipeline apps & context-aware links

A Pipeline app is any tool that implements Pipeline APIs — web, desktop, mobile, editor-integrated, or automated. A Pipeline link is an app-independent URL that lets teams move between apps without losing context.

1P

First-party

Built and distributed by Unity. User seat entitlement, subscription + consumption billing.

2P

Second-party

Built by a customer for their own needs, on our APIs.

3P

Third-party

Built by a customer and monetized outside their organization.

North Star

The whole team, their tools, and their agents — on the same project

  • Iteration time in seconds, not minutes.
  • All users in a studio collaborate around a project.
  • Multiple agents iterate on projects, with a human in the loop.
  • No setup time required.
  • API-first, enabling custom apps.
